Order of work

Sheet 6 / 6

Before assembly, panels flat

  1. Label every part with its cut list name.
  2. Edge band all visible edges.
  3. Drill all pocket holes from sheets 2–5. Jig at 3/4".
  4. Hinge cups, 35 mm: tall doors 4 each, top box doors 2 each.
  5. Fix the runners to the cabinet sides: A × 2, C × 3. The table drawer runners go on A's right side and C's left side.
  6. Shelf-pin holes in tower B. Rail socket holes at 1650 in A.

Assembly

  1. Lay out the base boards and level them with shims.
  2. Build A on its back: sides, top, bottom, fixed shelves. Check the diagonals, then screw on the back.
  3. Stand A on its base and screw it down. Do the same with B, leaving 1300 between them (use the table top to check).
  4. Plug A and B to the wall at the top so they can't tip.
  5. Plug the centre back panels to the wall. The seam goes behind the TV.
  6. Build C with no back. Set it on its base and screw it to B from inside C.
  7. Table top, then the table drawer.
  8. Divider, makeup shelves, TV ledge.
  9. Top boxes E and G: screw down into A and B. Then F, held up on a prop and screwed sideways into E and G (4 screws per side).
  10. Doors. Half-overlay hinges on the wall-side doors, full overlay on F. Set a 3 mm gap all round.
  11. Drawer fronts, mirror, rail, scribe strips, then the TV.
Glue: wood glue doesn't stick to laminate. If your boards are already laminated, use screws only, so you can still take the boxes apart. If they're plain plywood that you'll laminate later, glue and screw every fixed joint, and wipe off the squeeze-out.
